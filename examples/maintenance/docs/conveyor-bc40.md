# BC-40 Belt Conveyor: Maintenance Manual

Applies to the BC-40 flat belt conveyors that carry cased product from the packers to the palletiser.

## Specifications

The BC-40 uses a 600 mm wide, two-ply rubber belt running at 0.8 metres per second. It is driven by a 4 kW geared motor at the head pulley and tensioned by a screw take-up at the tail pulley. Carrying idlers are spaced at 1.2 metres and return idlers at 3 metres. An emergency pull cord runs along both sides of the conveyor for its full length.

## Daily Checks

Before the shift starts, walk the full length of the conveyor while it runs empty. Watch the belt edges at the head and tail pulleys; the belt should run within 25 mm of the pulley centre. Listen for squealing idlers, which indicate a seized bearing. Check that the belt scraper at the head pulley is in contact with the belt and that no product or debris has built up under the conveyor.

## Belt Tracking

A belt that runs to one side will damage its edges on the structure and can spill product. Always correct tracking with the conveyor running empty and with the guards in place. Start at the point where the belt first moves off centre and work in the direction of belt travel. Adjust the carrying idler just before the point of misalignment by moving one end of the idler forward in the direction of belt travel, about 3 mm at a time. The belt moves towards the end of the idler it touches first. Wait for at least three full belt revolutions between adjustments so the belt can settle. Do not track the belt by tilting the head or tail pulley, because that causes uneven tension across the belt and shortens its life.

## Belt Tension

Too little tension lets the drive pulley slip, especially at start-up under load. Too much tension overloads the pulley bearings and the belt splice. Set tension with the screw take-up so that the belt sags no more than 2 percent of the idler spacing between carrying idlers when loaded. Turn both take-up screws by the same amount so the tail pulley stays square to the frame.

## Splice Inspection

Inspect the mechanical belt splice every week. Look for missing or loose fasteners, cracks across the belt near the splice, and lifting of the splice plates. Replace the splice if more than two fasteners are missing or if a crack extends more than 10 percent of the belt width.

## Emergency Pull Cord Test

Test every emergency pull cord once a month. With the conveyor running, pull the cord at the midpoint of each side. The conveyor must stop within one second and must not restart until the cord switch is reset by hand at the switch and the start button is pressed. Record the result in the safety device log. A cord switch that fails the test must be repaired before the conveyor returns to service.

## Idler Replacement

Replace any idler that is seized, noisy or has a damaged shell. Isolate the conveyor using the lockout procedure first. Lift the belt off the idler with a belt lifter, never by hand, and remove the idler from its brackets. Fit the new idler, check that it turns freely by hand, and lower the belt. A single seized idler can wear a groove through the belt cover in a few days.
